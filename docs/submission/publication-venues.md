## Details

### 1. ICMDRSE 2027: 1st Int. Conf. on Multidisciplinary Research in Science & Engineering
- **Organiser:** Faculty of Science & Technology, Vishwakarma University, Pune. Hybrid, 8-9 July 2027.
- **Indexing:** Registered and presented papers go into IEEE conference proceedings and are submitted to IEEE Xplore, "subject to meeting IEEE quality and indexing requirements". This is the 1st edition, so it has no track record.
- **Dates:** CFP opened 10 Oct 2026. Submission 10 Jan 2027, notification 28 Feb 2027, registration 10 Mar 2027, camera-ready 10 Apr 2027.
- **Fees (incl. 18% GST):** IEEE student ₹8,260. Non-IEEE student ₹8,850. The site does not list what the fee covers or whether UG students qualify for the student rate. **Ask the organisers.**
- **Format:** Max 8 pages, PDF/Word, via CMT `https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ICMDRSE2027`.
- **Review papers:** Explicitly accepted ("review and survey papers" / "review articles").
- **Acceptance rate:** Not published.
- **Caution:** A search snippet said the conference sets a 10% similarity cap and does not allow AI-generated content. The official pages I opened did not show this, so check before you submit.
- **Links:** https://icmdrse.vupune.ac.in/callpaper.php, https://icmdrse.vupune.ac.in/Dates.php, https://icmdrse.vupune.ac.in/registration.php, https://icmdrse.vupune.ac.in/submission.php

- **Indexing:** "Accepted and presented papers will be published in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library". This is a long-running series (ESCI 2020 to 2026 are all in Xplore).

An aggregator (myhuiban) lists 15 Aug 2026 for 2027, but it is unofficial and possibly stale. **Email esciioit@aissmsioit.org to confirm the 2027 deadline.** If it has already closed, skip this one.
- **Fees:** IEEE student ₹5,000. Non-IEEE student ₹5,500. Non-presenting co-author certificate ₹500. The page does not say whether GST is included or what the fee covers.
- **Platform:** Microsoft CMT, `https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/ESCI2027`.
- **Review papers:** Not explicitly stated. The call invites papers with "literature reviews, problem statements, and methodologies". The 2026 call required less than 20% similarity.
- **Links:** https://aissmsioitesci.edu.in/, https://aissmsioitesci.edu.in/registration, https://enotice.vtools.ieee.org/public/182778 (IEEE eNotice for ESCI 2026)

### 4. I2CT 2027: 10th IEEE Int. Conf. for Convergence in Technology
- **Dates:** Full paper 15 Jan 2027 (the home page also mentions an extended hard deadline of 25 Jan), notification 15 Mar 2027, camera-ready 25 Apr 2027, conference 10-11 Oct 2027 (the home page says 11-12 Oct). The venue is not shown on the page I fetched.
- **Indexing:** Past papers are in IEEE Xplore and indexed in Scopus and WoS. The site cites an h-index of 12.
- **Fees:** Research scholar or full-time student author **₹9,000** (incl. 18% GST). Co-author or audience ₹6,000. This covers sessions, tea, lunch on the presentation day, and a certificate. Early registration ends 25 May 2027.
- **Drawback:** The conference is not until Oct 2027, so publication is about a year away.
- **Links:** https://www.i2ct.in/, https://www.i2ct.in/important-dates, https://www.i2ct.in/registration-fees

### 5. Journal of Scientific & Industrial Research (JSIR), CSIR-NIScPR (Govt. of India)
- **Indexing:** CiteScore 2.5 (Scopus) and JCR Impact Factor 0.8 (2024). The site also shows a SCImago ranking.
- **Cost:** "CSIR-NIScPR does not levy Article Processing Charges (APCs) or Article Submission Charges". It is open access under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
- **Review:** Double-blind with up to 3 experts. **Any negative report means rejection, and acceptance needs 2 positive reports.** It takes at least 4-6 weeks, and the journal's metrics list 12 days to first decision.
- **Scope:** Applied science and industry, including AI/ML, data science and digital transformation. "Purely theoretical work without industrial relevance is out of scope", so frame the paper around enterprise or workplace productivity.
- **Review papers:** Yes ("welcomes original research articles and reviews").
- **Verdict:** It is free and reputable, but **not easy**. Use it if the conferences fail.
- **Link:** https://or.niscpr.res.in/index.php/JSIR/about

## Predatory and quality warnings
- **Avoid** "publish in 2-7 days" Indian journals that charge ₹1,500-3,000 and offer certificates (the IJRASET, IJSREM and IJIRT style). Many claim fake impact factors or "UGC approved" status, and they do not count as reputed.
- **Avoid aggregator blogs** (blogspot "upcoming IEEE conferences" lists, theresearchgeeks, etc.) as sources of truth. Always submit from the official conference site.
- **Look-alike EasyChair domains** appeared in searches (`35wwwww.easychair.org`, `39www.easychair.org`, `1easychair-www.easychair.org`). Only use `easychair.org` or `cmt3.research.microsoft.com`.
- "IEEE Xplore" for a conference is always **"subject to IEEE approval"**. That is normal wording. A new 1st-edition event (ICMDRSE) carries more risk than an established series (ESCI, I2CT, ICCECE).
- Most of these conferences require an author to **register and present**, or the paper is dropped from Xplore.
- **Similarity and AI rules:** ESCI required less than 20% similarity, and ICMDRSE may be stricter (see above). arXiv's CS crackdown targeted AI-generated surveys, so make sure the review adds original synthesis such as a taxonomy, comparison tables or the Cortex case study, and is not just a list of papers.
- No venue above publishes an official acceptance rate except ICCECE's anecdotal 33% (2020). **Acceptance rates are unverified for all of them.**
